Neruluma Pack Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Neruluma Pack is an offline packing organizer. It stores journey names, destinations, dates, notes, luggage and checklist information in private local app storage. This app does not send this information to us. There are no accounts, advertisements, analytics SDKs or cloud databases. The app requests no network or sensitive permissions.

You control exports and imports using the Android system file picker. A chosen storage provider may upload files according to its own settings; choose on-device storage to keep exports offline. Backup files are not encrypted by the app. Delete exported copies separately. Android cloud backup and device transfer are excluded for private app data.

Delete a journey inside the app, or clear app storage or uninstall to remove all local records. We cannot recover local records remotely. Android's app sandbox protects stored files, but no device or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

If you contact navoshepley@gmail.com, we receive your address, message and attachments to respond to your request. Gmail and your mail provider process that correspondence. We retain it only as needed for support and applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion by emailing from the same address. Do not send unnecessary sensitive information.

Material changes to data handling will be disclosed in an updated policy and, where required, an additional notice. Privacy contact: navoshepley@gmail.com.
